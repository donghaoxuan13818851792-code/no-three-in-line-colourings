# G11 computational architecture

This file describes the intended proof architecture, not historical experimentation.

## R family

The R branch fixes multiple size-22 outer colour classes and reduces the remainder to a finite family of exact residual instances.

The final repository should record:

- the exact size-22 admissibility predicate;
- the disjointness/capacity filter;
- the symmetry quotient;
- the complete leaf universe;
- machine-checkable terminal certificates.

## Q family

All Q profiles contain a unique size-22 class. The common outer reduction is therefore a complete, symmetry-reduced catalogue of feasible size-22 anchors.

Recommended evidence layout:

- Q1–Q4: retain separate branch records unless their final certified formulations are genuinely identical;
- Q5–Q7: preserve the certified branch-specific decomposition;
- Q8–Q9: document the shared size-22/size-21 prefix universe and exact residual reduction;
- Q10: document its independent four-size-20-plus-remainder formulation and final global coverage.

## P family

The P family has no size-22 class and is organised naturally by the number of size-21 classes.

- P1–P3: early tight profiles;
- P4–P5: three interchangeable size-21 classes, with joint geometric/colour symmetry;
- P6: two interchangeable size-21 classes;
- P7: one size-21 class followed by an exact packing problem for five size-20 classes.

## Symmetry

All simultaneous geometric and equal-role colour symmetries must be applied jointly to complete tuples. Independent canonicalisation of components is not sufficient unless separately proved sound.

Every canonicalisation implementation should have:

- a mathematical equivalence relation;
- a deterministic canonical key;
- a coverage check;
- an independent orbit/canonicalisation audit where practical.
