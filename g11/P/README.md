# P family

Profiles with no size-22 colour class.

- [`P1–P3`](evidence/README.md) are complete: primary and independent six-shard runs agree across 89 fixed representatives and 708,638 residual instances, with no residual SAT result.
- P4–P5 use three size-21 classes; P6 and P7 have separate remaining evidence obligations.

The common substrate is the complete admissible size-21 catalogue. Later profiles are organized by decreasing numbers of size-21 outer classes.
